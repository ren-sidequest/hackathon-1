Harbour Retail — Junior Data Analyst Demo JD 
Junior Data Analyst 
Company: Harbour Retail 
Location: Sydney, NSW 
Employment Type: Full-time 
Experience Level: Entry Level / Junior 
Team: Data & Analytics 
About Harbour Retail 
Harbour Retail is a growing Sydney-based e-commerce startup using data to improve 
customer acquisition, digital shopping experiences and commercial performance. 
As the business grows, we are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to support the team in 
turning website marketing and sales data into clear insights that help teams make 
better decisions. 
You will work with members of the Data, Marketing and Commercial teams and will be 
supported by more experienced analysts. This role is suited to a recent graduate or 
early-career analyst who enjoys working with data, investigating business questions and 
communicating findings clearly. 
About the Role 
As a Junior Data Analyst, you will help collect, validate, analyses and interpret business 
data across website traffic, marketing channels and online sales. 
Your work will include routine reporting as well as ad hoc investigations into changes in 
business performance. 
A key part of the role is not only calculating metrics, but understanding what those 
metrics mean, identifying when further evidence is required, and communicating 
findings to non-technical stakeholders. 
Key Responsibilities 
Data Preparation and Quality 
• Collect, organize and validate data from sales, website and marketing sources. 
• Check datasets for missing values, inconsistencies and obvious data-quality 
issues. 
• Maintain clear documentation of data sources, calculations and analytical 
assumptions. 
• Support reliable and consistent reporting across business teams. 
SQL and Data Analysis

• Write basic SQL queries to extract, filter, group and summaries business data. 
• Use aggregation techniques to analyses performance across products, time 
periods and acquisition channels. 
• Calculate and monitor key business metrics such as website traffic, orders and 
conversion rates. 
• Compare results across periods or customer/channel segments to identify 
meaningful changes. 
Business Analysis and Problem Solving 
• Investigate changes in business performance and identify possible explanations 
supported by available data. 
• Distinguish between what the data directly shows and what remains uncertain. 
• Identify when additional data is required before reaching a business conclusion. 
• Perform ad hoc analysis to support questions raised by Marketing, Product or 
Commercial teams. 
• Translate analytical findings into practical next steps or recommendations where 
the available evidence supports them. 
Reporting and Visualization 
• Assist in creating recurring reports, tables and dashboards using Excel or 
business-intelligence tools. 
• Present metrics and analytical findings in a clear, structured and accessible 
format. 
• Support the development of simple visualizations that help stakeholders 
understand trends and performance. 
Stakeholder Communication 
• Explain analytical findings clearly to colleagues who may not have a technical 
background. 
• Summaries the business implications of an analysis rather than only presenting 
calculations. 
• Clearly communicate assumptions, limitations and areas where further 
investigation is required. 
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand business questions and 
data requirements.

What Good Looks Like in This Role 
A successful Junior Data Analyst does more than produce correct calculations. 
You should be able to: 
• identify the right metric for a business question; 
• analyses data accurately; 
• recognize when a conclusion is or is not supported by the available evidence; 
• explain your reasoning clearly; 
• identify what additional information may be required; and 
• turn analysis into a sensible next step for the business. 
Example Business Questions You May Work On 
• Why has website conversion changed even though traffic has increased? 
• Which acquisition channels appear to be driving changes in customer behavior?

What We Value 
We value candidates who are curious, evidence-driven and comfortable saying when 
the available data is not sufficient to support a conclusion. 
For a junior role, we do not expect you to know everything. We are interested in how 
you approach a problem, how carefully you work with evidence, and how clearly you 
explain your reasoning.
