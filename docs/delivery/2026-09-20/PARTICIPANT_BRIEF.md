**Internal formative study · fictional applications · synthetic work samples**

We are testing a review workflow, not you. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time. We record anonymous notes and timing; no recording of your face, voice, real CV or contact details is needed. Tell the facilitator if you need an accessibility adjustment before the clock starts.

## Your task

You are reviewing applications for the Junior Data Analyst job in the supplied job description.

1. Select **two people worth learning more about** from the four fictional candidates.
2. For each selected person, write **two supported reasons**, giving the source file and a page, line range or short exact quotation that supports each reason.
3. For each selected person, write **one point you would check next**.

There is no prescribed pair of people to select. We assess whether your reasoning is supported by the material, not whether you match someone else's hiring preference. You are not making a hiring decision about real people.

## Materials and working conditions

Everyone receives the same job description, four fictional CVs and eight clearly labelled synthetic work samples. The unchanged original PDFs and searchable text are in `materials.html`. You may use normal browser Find, PDF search, notes and any reading order. External searches, external AI assistance and another person's help interpreting the candidates are outside this short test for both groups.

- **Raw-material group:** work from the shared material page and original files.
- **Prototype group:** use the prepared EvidenceBridge prototype and the same shared material page. Its reports are preloaded and AI-authored, with human calibration pending. It is in manual rule-simulation mode; this test does not assess a live model. Browse and compare, but record your answer outside the prototype. Leave send, submission, review and shortlist-save controls untouched.

You have up to **20 minutes**, including reading, source checking and writing your final answer. The facilitator gives the same neutral two-minute orientation to each participant before timing starts. Materials first become available when timing starts. If you finish earlier, say **“Done”**. The timer continues during help and interruptions; both are recorded. At the time limit, we keep your partial response rather than treating it as a completed task. If you stop, your partial response is labelled accordingly.

Ask the facilitator for navigation or technical help when needed. They will log the help and avoid interpreting the evidence or suggesting a person. You may state any difficulty or uncertainty in your response.
