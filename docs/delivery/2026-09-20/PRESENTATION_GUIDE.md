## 2. 全组共用的产品事实

- 公司/岗位：Harbour Retail / Junior Data Analyst。
- 候选人：Amy Chen、Ann Li、David Liu、Jamie Parker，四人同规则。均为合成演示身份。
- 材料：用户提供的合成JD和4份CV，另有8份明确标注的合成项目附件。个人项目与公司补证任务的数据分开。
- 主用户与付费方：招聘资源有限的小企业的招聘负责人/用人经理；候选人免费。
- 默认先读已有CV和作品。关键证据缺失时才请求澄清或定向任务；每人至多一个任务，V1加最多一次V2。
- 核心评分：SQL / Data Analysis / Business Problem Solving，10项标准、30/30/40权重。完整JD其他要求单独展示。
- 当前运行模式保持规则模拟。不要描述为现场上传任意CV后实时大模型自动评估。
- 人工评分、证据审核、名单保留是独立动作；名单不是录用或自动淘汰。
- 所有PPT标题、图表、截图和上台讲稿用英文。本指南中文是内部协作用。

## 4. 必须讲清的一项评分例

### 标准与分数的含义

| 技能 | 标准摘要 | 权重 |
|---|---|---:|
| SQL | S1粒度/聚合/连接；S2时间与分组比较；S3检查方法和边界 | 30% |
| Data Analysis | D1指标计算；D2有意义的比较；D3复核依据与限制 | 30% |
| Business Problem Solving | B1问题/决策；B2观察与因果；B3区分假设的数据请求；B4行动优先级与验证 | 40% |

每项Mark 0–4，贡献为Mark÷4×10。权重是团队的demo政策，并非JD提供的权重。NE表示材料不足以判断，不是0分；缺证时保留不完整状态，不强造综合百分比。

**展开例：Amy B3。** 目前基准例给Mark 2，即5/10：能提出具体拆分数据请求，但比较方法和不同结果会支持/削弱什么解释仍不足。展示顺序是标准、原文、判断理由、分数贡献、下一步追问。最终以复核后的当前版本为准。

页上可用：

> The score summarises evidence against our core rubric. Other job requirements remain visible separately.

截图保留原文来源和必要上下文；证据为静态SQL/项目文件时，不把阅读检查说成已实际执行代码。

## 6. 竞品、收费和收益怎么讲

### 竞品

沿用总方案的研究对象：TestGorilla、Vervoe、Greenhouse Real Talent。**展示前由B核对当前官方页面，不把旧研究当成今天已验证。**

比较目标用户、默认输入、证据解释/人工控制、追问流程、上手负担。待核实的项写“not verified”，不填“No”冒充缺失功能。

- [TestGorilla官方入口](https://www.testgorilla.com/pricing/)
- [Vervoe官方入口](https://vervoe.com/assessments/)
- [Greenhouse官方入口](https://www.greenhouse.com/real-talent-candidate-matching)

保留三家对照表，主讲只说最关键差异。避免“只有我们可解释”“其他产品全是黑箱”或“面向SME就是独家”。我们的拟议区别是小团队、初级分析岗、已有作品优先、证据/未知与针对性追问的一套轻量工作流程；实际效果仍待验证。

### 收费

英文包名可用 `Single-role evidence review package`。

雇主按岗位＋约定候选人批次购买：标准确认、材料证据对照、缺口与追问、候选人比较、人工名单和可交接报告。候选人免费；支付系统及任意人数批量处理不在本次demo承诺中。

> Employers pay per role and agreed candidate batch. Pricing and batch size will be tested in a pilot.

### 收益与数字

重点是少翻文件、少遗漏、追问更具体、交接更清楚。实际节约以审阅时间、引用正确率、遗漏及额外复核成本衡量。

原第8页约A$59是小时人力成本参考，不是售价。使用前补可直达的来源、时间和人群定义；未核实就移除。若使用总方案A$320例，显著标 `Illustrative assumptions, not measured savings`，附40份×(15−7)分钟×A$60/小时，并说明还未扣软件费及额外成本，也不意味着当前demo支持40人。

没有真实试用结果时直接用：

> Savings have not yet been measured. Our next pilot will compare review time and evidence quality, including setup and human review.

不承诺提升留任、减少试用期离职或替代整个HR团队。长期效果是后续企业试点的问题。

## 7. 当前模式统一英文说明

> This prototype uses prepared fictional cases, pre-authored demo assessments and rule-based evidence analysis. Scores follow a fixed rubric, and people make the final decision. No live model call is used in this demo.

网站模式、PPT正文、讲者备注都一致。初始AI编写评估与真人复核是两回事；真人尚未签收就保留 `Human calibration pending`，部分复核就说明范围。

## 8. 答辩速查（指定一人主答，其他人补充）

| 评委问题 | 建议英文回答 |
|---|---|
| Why would an employer pay? | We aim to reduce time spent locating evidence and preparing follow-up questions. The employer buys a review package for one role. A pilot will test the time savings and willingness to pay. |
| What if applicants only have a CV? | We show what the CV claims and what remains unverified. The reviewer can request relevant existing work or a focused clarification. This demo uses fixed documents rather than a general CV parser. |
| What does the percentage mean? | It summarises evidence for our SQL, data analysis and business problem-solving rubric. It does not predict hiring success or retention. We show other job requirements separately. |
| Why does someone have NE? | NE means the available material does not support a judgment. A zero means we observed a specific problem against the criterion. |
| Who sets the marks? | The demo starts with pre-authored judgments linked to source material. Reviewers can revise judgments. The software calculates the weighted totals. We report human review status separately. |
| Can the recruiter keep someone outside the top five? | Yes. The reviewer can inspect every applicant and retain someone for further discussion. Our four-person demo illustrates that control without automatic rejection. |
| Must every applicant take a test? | No. We start with existing evidence and request additional work only for an important gap. A targeted task allows an initial submission and at most one follow-up. |
| Where is the AI? | The current demo uses prepared assessments and rule-based analysis. Live model integration is outside this hackathon delivery. The demo shows the evidence workflow and human controls. |
| What makes you different? | We focus on small teams hiring junior analysts, with existing work as the starting point and specific gaps guiding follow-up. We still need to test whether this workflow is easier and more useful than existing options. |
| Have you proved savings or better hires? | We separate engineering tests, internal usability findings and future employer outcomes. We only report measured results where we have records. |
| Can this cover other roles? | The workflow may transfer, but each role needs appropriate criteria and validation. This prototype covers one junior data analyst role. |
| What is the moat? | Our next step is to test role-specific evidence standards and reviewer consistency with employers. We do not claim an established data moat from four fictional cases. |
